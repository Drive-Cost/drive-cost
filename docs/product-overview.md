# DriveCost

DriveCost is a local-first mobile product that helps people understand the
cost and use of the vehicles they own. It turns routine vehicle events—such
as a fill-up, charge, repair, or ownership expense—into clear, explainable
information that supports better ownership decisions.

The product is for everyday drivers who want confidence in what their vehicle
costs, and for enthusiasts who value a richer ownership record. In both cases,
the experience should be quick to use, useful without an account or network
connection, and honest about what the recorded data can and cannot show.

DriveCost is not simply an expense ledger. Its value comes from connecting
low-friction recording with trustworthy cost and usage explanations, then using
those explanations to help a person decide what to do next.
