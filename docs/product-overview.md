# DriveCost — True Cost of Car Ownership

## Overview

DriveCost is a mobile application designed to help users understand the **real cost of owning a car**.

Most people track fuel or maintenance separately, but very few tools provide a **complete view of car ownership cost** including:

- fuel
- maintenance
- insurance
- taxes
- cost per kilometer
- yearly cost insights

DriveCost aims to provide a **simple and powerful dashboard** showing the true cost of owning and operating a vehicle.

---

# Target Users

## 1. Everyday Drivers

Users who want a simple overview of their car expenses.

Goals:

- Track fuel spending
- Track maintenance costs
- Understand monthly car expenses
- See cost per km

---

## 2. Car Enthusiasts

Users who enjoy tracking detailed information about their cars.

Goals:

- Record full maintenance history
- Track modifications and upgrades
- Compare vehicle costs
- Export reports

This group is more likely to pay for premium features.

---

# Core Features (MVP)

## Garage

Users can add and manage vehicles.

Vehicle data:

- Brand
- Model
- Year
- Current mileage

---

## Fuel Tracking

Users can record fuel fill-ups.

Data recorded:

- Date
- Liters
- Price
- Mileage

The app calculates:

- fuel consumption
- cost per kilometer

If the vehicle is electric, the same entry flow is used for charge sessions and
energy cost tracking.

---

## Maintenance Tracking

Users can record maintenance and repairs.

Data recorded:

- Type (oil change, tires, repairs, etc.)
- Cost
- Date
- Mileage

---

## Dashboard

Provides an overview of vehicle costs.

Metrics:

- Total cost
- Monthly cost
- Cost per kilometer
- Fuel vs maintenance spending
- EV-aware or fuel-aware copy and entry terminology

Example dashboard:

Audi A4 1.9 TDI  
Total yearly cost: €3240  
Cost per km: €0.36
